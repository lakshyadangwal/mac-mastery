# 08 — Trackpad Gestures

Your keyboard is across the room. Your trackpad is right under your fingers — learn its gestures and you might never need the mouse again.

> Some gestures (like three-finger drag) need to be turned on in **System Settings > Accessibility > Pointer Control > Trackpad Options**.

## Gestures

| Gesture | What it does | Level | Memory hook |
|---------|--------------|-------|-------------|
| Two-finger swipe left/right | Go back / forward in browser | Beginner | Two fingers turn the page like a book |
| Two-finger scroll | Scroll up and down | Beginner | Slide two fingers to move the page |
| Three-finger swipe left/right | Switch between full-screen apps | Power User | Three fingers hop between your desktops |
| Four-finger swipe up | Mission Control | Beginner | Four fingers throw every window into view |
| Four-finger swipe down | App Exposé | Power User | Four fingers down showers one app's windows |
| Thumb + three-finger pinch | Show Desktop / Launchpad | Beginner | Pinch to clear the desk and see everything |
| Three-finger tap | Look up a word or data detector | Power User | Tap with three fingers to learn what something is |
| Two-finger tap | Right-click | Beginner | Two fingers is your right-click |
| Two-finger pinch open | Zoom in | Beginner | Spread your fingers to make it bigger |
| Two-finger pinch close | Zoom out | Beginner | Pinch to make it smaller |
| Four-finger swipe left/right | Move between Spaces | Power User | Four fingers glide between desktops |
| Three-finger drag | Drag items without clicking | Power User | Three fingers grab and move (enable in Accessibility) |
| Thumb + three-finger spread | Launchpad | Beginner | Spread to see all your apps at once |

## Try it now

1. Swipe up with four fingers to open Mission Control
2. Swipe left or right with three fingers to change desktops
3. Pinch with your thumb and three fingers to show the desktop
